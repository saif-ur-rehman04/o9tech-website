We built Infinite Geometry Stack to be simple and privacy friendly. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email, or any similar personal details.

01 Information stored on your device

The Game saves a small amount of data on your device so it can remember things between sessions. This includes your high scores and game progress, your sound and other settings, and your privacy choices. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is removed if you delete the App.

02 Advertising

Infinite Geometry Stack is free to play and is supported by ads. We use Google AdMob, along with Google's advertising partners, to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads inside the Game.

To show ads, these services may automatically collect and process certain information, such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier;
  • Your IP address and general location based on it;
  • Your device type, operating system, and language;
  • How you interact with ads.

This information is used to serve, measure, and improve ads. We do not receive or store this information ourselves. It is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which you can read at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

03 Personalized ads and your consent

Depending on where you live, when you first open the Game you may see a consent message asking whether you agree to personalized ads. This message is provided through Google's consent tools. You can accept or decline.

If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change your choice at any time from the settings screen inside the Game, where a privacy options link appears when it applies to your region.

04 Your choices and controls

You have control over ad tracking through your device settings:

  • Android: Open Settings, then Google, then Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads.
  • iOS: Open Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Tracking, where you can control whether apps may ask to track you.

You can also clear all locally stored game data at any time by deleting the App.

05 Children's privacy

Infinite Geometry Stack is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Where required, ads shown to users we understand to be children are limited to non-personalized ads.

06 Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties except for the advertising services described above, which operate under their own privacy policies.

07 Data retention

Game data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. Information processed by our advertising partners is retained according to their own policies.

08 Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. Because the Game keeps your game data on your own device and does not send it to us, the main protection is the security of your device itself.

09 International users

The Game is available in many countries. Advertising partners may process information in countries other than your own. By playing, you understand that information handled by these partners may be processed in line with their privacy policies.

10 Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California. These can include the right to access, correct, or delete information, and the right to object to certain processing.

Because we do not collect or hold personal information about you ourselves, most requests of this kind relate to the advertising partners. You can use many of these controls directly through the device and consent settings described above, and you may also contact us using the details below.

11 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top. We encourage you to review this page from time to time.
