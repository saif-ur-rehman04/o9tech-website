FAQ

Native iOS & Android questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

When is native the right choice?

When the app leans on the device: NFC, Bluetooth, Screen Time, VoIP, Apple Pay, background location or heavy animation. Native also suits apps where speed and security matter most, like banking. For simpler apps, a cross-platform build can reach both stores faster.

Can you take over an existing iOS or Android app?

Yes. About 40% of our recent work is taking over codebases other teams started, then stabilizing and shipping them. We start with a code review and a written plan.

Do you handle App Store and Google Play releases?

Yes. We prepare the builds, store listings and review notes, and we deal with any rejections until the app is live.
