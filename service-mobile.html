What we do

How we help

When an app has to be quick, secure and deeply tied to the device, native is the right call. We build with Swift and SwiftUI on iOS and Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on Android, and we know the hard parts: Screen Time, NFC, VoIP, Apple Pay, background location and offline maps.

  • SwiftUI and UIKit apps, including large migrations to SwiftUI
  • Kotlin and Jetpack Compose apps with clean, modular architecture
  • Banking-grade security: SSL pinning, keychain, encrypted storage
  • Hardware work: NFC, Bluetooth, thermal printers, Screen Time
  • App Store and Google Play releases, reviews and rejections handled
