The product

What we built

JavaKotlinGoogle MapsREST APIs

Gravel was a Los Angeles parking marketplace, the Airbnb of parking. Drivers found and paid for private spots, and owners rented out driveways they were not using.

We built key Android features for hosts and drivers: real-time spot availability, custom pricing and hours, calendar management and secure payments.

  • Map search for private spots nearby
  • Hosts set their own price and hours
  • Book and pay in the app
  • Payouts to hosts after checkout
