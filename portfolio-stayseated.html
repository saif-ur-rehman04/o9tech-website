Stay Seated
The product

What we built

KotlinJavaPayment gateway

Stay Seated was an in-venue ordering app from Milwaukee. Fans ordered from concessions and a runner brought it to their seat.

We built the Android app in Kotlin and Java with a payment gateway, so an order went straight from the menu to the seat.

  • Order from concession menus
  • Runners deliver to your seat
  • Live order tracking
  • Deals sent to your phone
