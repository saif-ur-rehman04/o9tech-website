How Pigeon Post works

  • Real time flights

    From 2 minutes to 8 hours. They land even with the app closed.

  • A postcard for every trip

    Each one is drawn for that trip, with one dry line about what happened.

  • A stamp album

    A common and a rare stamp for each of 12 cities.

No account. Your progress stays on your phone.

Privacy and support

Pigeon Post has no account and no sign-in. Questions about the game? A person reads every message.
