01 Who This App Is For

SnoozeTale generates personalized bedtime stories for children ages 2–10. It's intended to be downloaded, set up, and operated by a parent or legal guardian on behalf of their child. If you are a parent or guardian setting this app up for a child, you are the one agreeing to these Terms, and you're responsible for how the app is used on your device.

02 The Service

SnoozeTale lets you create a story for your child by choosing a theme, moral, mood, length, and narrator voice; the app generates a unique story using AI, and, for Premium subscribers, professionally-styled narrated audio. One story per day is available for free, narrated by your device's built-in voice. A Premium subscription unlocks unlimited stories, two additional cinematic narrator voices, and a permanently saved story library.

Stories are generated automatically by an AI system. While we apply content-safety checks intended to keep stories gentle and age-appropriate (no scary, violent, or otherwise unsettling content), we cannot guarantee that every generated story will be perfect for every child every time. Please review a story yourself if you have any concern before sharing it with your child, and stop using a story or theme that doesn't feel right for your family.
