SnoozeTale creates personalized, AI-generated bedtime stories for children. It's designed to be used by parents and guardians on behalf of their children. We built SnoozeTale to collect as little as possible. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no email address required to use the app.

01 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required info to generate and track your sleep stories:

  • Anonymous device identifier. The app generates a random identifier on your device to keep track of your free daily story and, if you subscribe, your premium status. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information, and is not shared with advertisers.
  • Your child's first name (transient only). If you enter your child's first name to personalize a story, that name is used only to generate that one story. It is sent to our story-generation service for that single request and is never stored, logged, or saved on our servers, and is never linked to your device identifier in any record we keep. Your child's name only lives on your own device, in the profile you create there.
  • Story preferences. Each time you request a story, the app sends your chosen settings for that story — theme, moral, mood, length, narrator voice, and your child's age range — to our backend so it can generate the story. These preferences are not linked to any personally identifying information.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe to SnoozeTale Premium, our payment partner (RevenueCat) manages your subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat receives your anonymous device identifier and purchase/entitlement status — never your child's information.

02 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, physical address, precise location, contacts, photos, or any advertising identifiers (the app never reads your device's advertising identifier, and never asks for tracking permission). We do not run any analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell data to anyone. The free version shows a small number of non-personalized ads (see Section 7a); the advertising provider never receives your child's name, your device's advertising identifier, or anything usable to build a profile of you or your child.

03 What's Stored on Your Device

Your child's profiles (name, age range), your saved story library (text and any downloaded premium narration), app settings, and your bedtime reminder time are all stored locally on your device only. None of this is backed up to our servers or to any cloud account.

You can permanently delete all of it at any time from Settings → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app.

04 How Story Generation Works

When you request a story, the app sends your story preferences (and, if provided, your child's first name for that story only) to our backend, which uses OpenAI's services to write the story text and, for Premium narrators, generate audio narration. OpenAI processes this information solely to produce that one story and does not retain it on our behalf beyond that.

Premium narration audio is temporarily stored in cloud storage so your device can download it, then automatically deleted shortly afterward — it is not kept indefinitely.

05 Third-Party Services We Use

We integrate with a small set of secure third-party services to deliver personalized story content:

  • OpenAI — generates story text and premium narration audio, from the preferences and (if provided) child's first name sent for that one request.
  • Firebase (Google Cloud) — our backend infrastructure. Firestore stores only an anonymous daily-usage counter keyed to your device identifier; Cloud Storage temporarily hosts generated audio files before they're deleted.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play, using your anonymous device identifier.
  • Google AdMob (free version only) — displays a banner ad on the app's browsing screens. Every ad request is flagged as child-directed, which contractually and technically disables personalized ads, remarketing, and the transmission of your device's advertising identifier; ads are restricted to a "G" (general audiences) content rating and are contextual only. Google receives standard network information (such as IP address) to deliver the ad. Subscribers see no ads at all, and no ad requests are made from their devices.

None of these services receive your child's name outside the single, transient story-generation request described above.

06 Notifications

If you turn on a bedtime reminder, it's scheduled entirely on your own device using your device's local notification system. We do not operate a push notification server and cannot send you notifications remotely.

07 Children's Privacy

SnoozeTale is intended to be operated by a parent or guardian on behalf of their child, not directly by a child. Settings, purchases, and any links that leave the app are placed behind a parental gate (a simple challenge intended to keep young children from accessing them unsupervised).

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and we do not permit children to create accounts, since the app has no account system at all. The one piece of information a parent might enter that identifies a child — their first name — is deliberately never stored on our servers, exactly to minimize any privacy exposure for that information.

The free version of the app shows non-personalized, child-appropriate advertising as described in Section 7a. We use no tracking technology of any kind: no behavioral profiles, no advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking.

07a Advertising (Free Version Only)

The free version displays a single banner ad on the app's browsing screens (Home, Library, and Settings). Ads never appear during story playback or on any screen designed for a child's eyes at bedtime.

Every ad request is made in Google AdMob's child-directed mode: ads are contextual (based on nothing about you — not your interests, not your history), restricted to a G content rating, and your device's advertising identifier is never transmitted. We never show ads based on personal information, and we never ask for tracking permission because nothing in the app tracks you.

If you ever see an ad you consider inappropriate, please report it via Settings → Report an inappropriate ad and we will escalate it to the ad provider.

SnoozeTale Premium removes advertising entirely.

08 Security

Your child's profile and story data is stored using your device's local, encrypted app storage. Communication between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit. Because we don't operate user accounts, there's no password or login credential of yours for us to protect or that could be compromised in a server-side breach involving your personal identity.

09 Your Choices

  • Delete everything the app has stored locally at any time via Settings → Delete All Data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data along with it.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription anytime through the Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings, or via Settings → Manage Subscription in the app.
  • Contact us (below) with any privacy question or request.
