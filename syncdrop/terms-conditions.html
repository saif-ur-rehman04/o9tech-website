01 Description of Service

SyncDrop is a utility app that allows users to transfer files directly between devices over a shared local Wi-Fi network, using local network discovery (mDNS/Bonjour) and a temporary on-device connection. The App does not route, store, or process files through any server operated by us — transfers occur solely between the sending and receiving devices.
