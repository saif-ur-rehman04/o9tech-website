The short version: SyncDrop transfers files directly between devices over your local Wi-Fi network. There are no SyncDrop servers in the middle — we never see, receive, or store the files you send. There's no account, no sign-up, and no email address required to use the app. Almost everything this policy covers relates to two things we don't control directly: the advertising SDK (Google AdMob) and the app stores that process your purchases.

01 Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be specific here, because "we care about your privacy" is meaningless without specifics:

  • We do not require or collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials — SyncDrop has no account system.
  • We do not receive, store, or have any technical ability to access the files you transfer. Transfers happen device-to-device over your local network; no SyncDrop server exists to route them through.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not maintain any server-side log of your transfers, contacts, or device names.

02 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To function, SyncDrop stores the following data only on your own device — it is never transmitted to us:

  • Transfer history — a local log of past sends/receives (filename, size, timestamp, peer device name), shown in the app's History screen. You can clear this at any time in Settings.
  • App settings — your device name, preferred save location, and toggle preferences (e.g., auto-accept from known devices).

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data, since none of it exists anywhere else.

03 Permissions the App Requests

None of these permissions give us — the developer — remote access to your device. They only enable on-device functionality:

Permission Why We Ask
Local Network access (iOS) / Nearby Wi-Fi Devices (Android) Required to discover other SyncDrop devices on your Wi-Fi network via mDNS/Bonjour. Without it, the app cannot find peers.
Wi-Fi state (Android) Used to confirm you're connected to a network before scanning.
File / storage access Required to let you select files to send and to save files you receive.
Notifications (optional) Used only to alert you to an incoming file request. You can decline this and still use the app fully.

04 Third-Party Services

Google AdMob. SyncDrop is free to use and supported by ads shown via Google AdMob, which appear after a completed transfer. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. This collection is performed by Google, governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by us directly. On iOS, we request App Tracking Transparency permission before enabling personalized ads, per Apple's requirements — you can decline this and still see (non-personalized) ads.

Apple App Store / Google Play. In-app purchases (SyncDrop Pro, Remove Ads) are billed and processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your payment card details — the platform handles the transaction and shares only the purchase status with our app, which we use to unlock the corresponding feature locally on your device.

05 Do We Sell Your Data?

No. We do not sell any data. Note that under some regional laws (e.g., California's CPRA), the use of an advertising SDK like AdMob can be legally classified as "sharing" data with a third party for cross-context advertising purposes, even though no money changes hands. Where required, we provide the opt-out controls described in Section 7.

06 Data Retention

Local data (history, settings) persists on your device until you clear it in Settings or uninstall the app — entirely under your control. We have no server-side copy to retain or delete, because none exists.

07 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR or the California CPRA, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data, and to opt out of targeted advertising.

  • Local app data: You can view, clear, or delete this at any time via Settings → Clear Transfer History, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Advertising data (AdMob): Manage ad personalization via your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization), or through Google's own controls at adssettings.google.com.
  • Questions or requests: Contact us using the details in Section 11.

08 Children's Privacy

SyncDrop is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will address it.

09 Security

Because transfers happen only over your local Wi-Fi network — never over the public internet — your files are not exposed to us or any third party during transfer. As with any local network, only devices you've allowed onto your Wi-Fi can discover or receive transfers via SyncDrop.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
