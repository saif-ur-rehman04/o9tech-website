How we work

A clear plan, weekly builds, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your goals, users and constraints, then agree on scope, platforms and a realistic timeline. You get a written plan, not a guess.

  2. 02

    Design & architect

    We map the screens and the system behind them: data models, APIs and the stack that fits. Big decisions get made early and on paper.

  3. 03

    Build in sprints

    Working builds every week on TestFlight, Play internal testing or a staging URL. You see real progress and steer as we go.

  4. 04

    Launch & scale

    We handle store submission, monitoring and the first rounds of fixes, then stay on to grow the product with you.

FAQ

Cloud & DevOps questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Can you set up CI/CD for our mobile apps?

Yes. We automate builds and releases with Fastlane for native apps and EAS for Expo apps, so every change can reach TestFlight or Play testing without manual steps.

AWS or Google Cloud?

Both work well. We usually follow what your team already uses, and we size the setup to your traffic so you do not pay for servers you do not need.

Do you set up monitoring?

Yes. Crash reporting, logging, uptime checks and alerts are part of every setup, so problems show up before your users report them.
