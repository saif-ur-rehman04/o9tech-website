How we help

We use blockchain when you need a record nobody can quietly change: verifiable voting, payments and digital ownership. We build the contracts and the apps people use to reach them, with a sharp focus on security and user experience.

How we work

A clear plan, weekly builds, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your goals, users and constraints, then agree on scope, platforms and a realistic timeline. You get a written plan, not a guess.

  2. 02

    Design & architect

    We map the screens and the system behind them: data models, APIs and the stack that fits. Big decisions get made early and on paper.

  3. 03

    Build in sprints

    Working builds every week on TestFlight, Play internal testing or a staging URL. You see real progress and steer as we go.

  4. 04

    Launch & scale

    We handle store submission, monitoring and the first rounds of fixes, then stay on to grow the product with you.

FAQ

Blockchain questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

When does a product really need blockchain?

When you need a record nobody can quietly change, like verifiable votes, payments or digital ownership. If a normal database does the job, we will tell you.

Which chains do you work with?

Ethereum, Solana, Polygon and BSV, with smart contracts written in Solidity and Rust.

How do you handle smart contract security?

Every contract gets test coverage and a security review before it goes live.
