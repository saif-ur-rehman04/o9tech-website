How Hue Pour works

  • Pigments mix like real paint

    Mixing uses a Kubelka-Munk pigment model, so blue and yellow make green and red and white make pink.

  • Scored on true color distance

    Your match is scored with CIEDE2000, the measure color scientists use for how different two colors look to a person.

  • One Daily Palette swatch a day

    Every day there is one swatch to mix, the same for everyone. Match it to keep your streak going.
