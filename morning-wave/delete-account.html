Morning Wave

Delete your account and data

Morning Wave: Family Check-In · Effective 27 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Morning Wave account and data, for the app Morning Wave: Family Check-In by o9Tech. You can do it in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

Delete in the app

Caregivers and backup contacts

  1. Open Morning Wave and go to the Circle tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Circle owners

Before deleting your account, either transfer ownership to another caregiver (Circle tab, then Transfer ownership), or delete the circle (Circle tab, then Delete circle). Then follow the steps above.

Parents

On the parent's phone, open Settings and tap Disconnect this phone, then confirm twice. This deletes the phone's anonymous account. The family can also delete the whole circle from a caregiver's phone.

Delete without the app

Email support@o9tech.com with the subject "Delete my Morning Wave account", from the email address you use to sign in (or tell us which Google or Apple account you use).

We will confirm the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number and photo.
  • Your circle memberships, notification settings and devices, including push tokens.
  • Your subscription record in Morning Wave.
  • If you delete a circle: everything in it, including the parent's details and photo, check-ins, moods, medicines, appointments, notes, pauses, alerts and history. The parent's phone is disconnected.

What is kept, and for how long

  • In circles you leave but do not delete, earlier actions you took (such as resolving an alert) stay in the circle's history labelled "Former member", without your name or contact details.
  • Activity timeline entries expire after 90 days and notification logs after 30 days.
  • Encrypted backups are overwritten on their normal cycle, within 30 days.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store keep their own purchase records under their own policies.
Subscriptions are not cancelled automatically. Deleting your account does not stop a Morning Wave Family subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions) or in your Apple ID subscription settings.
