Morning Wave: Family Check-In · Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information Morning Wave collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. Morning Wave is provided by o9Tech ("we", "us"), Office No. 8, 3rd Floor, Abbas Trade Center, Lahore, Pakistan. Questions: support@o9tech.com.

The short version. Morning Wave never asks for anyone's location or contacts. A parent's check-ins, mood and medicines are shared only with the family members and backup contacts in that parent's circle. We do not sell personal information. You can delete your account and your data at any time.

1. How Morning Wave works

A family creates a circle for one parent. The parent's phone is set up with a one-time pairing code and gets an anonymous account, with no email or password. Each morning the parent taps "I'm OK". The family members in the circle ("caregivers") see the check-in, and if it is missed they receive alerts, followed by a trusted "backup contact" if needed. Caregivers sign in with Google, Apple or email.

Please only create a circle for a parent who knows about it and agrees to it. Morning Wave is built on the parent choosing to check in, not on watching them.

3. Health information

Mood and medicine information is health-related. We collect it only when a family chooses to use those features, use it only to run the service for that circle, and never use it for advertising. You can delete it at any time by removing the medicine, deleting the circle or deleting your account.

4. Who can see your information

  • Members of a circle see the parent's status, check-in times, mood, medicines, appointments, notes and photo, and each other's names. Caregivers see each other's phone numbers so they can call one another.
  • The parent's phone shows caregivers' first names and the primary contact's phone number, for the Call and Text buttons.
  • Backup contacts receive later-stage alerts and help alerts for the circles they belong to.
  • Invite links open a web page that shows no names, photos or circle details.
  • Service providers that run parts of Morning Wave for us, under contracts that limit their use of the data (section 5).
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Advertising

Caregivers on the free plan may see a small banner ad on some information screens. Ads never appear on the parent's phone, during an alert or help request, or for anyone in a premium circle. We request non-personalised ads only. Google AdMob still processes your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate a general location), and basic interaction and diagnostic data to show ads, limit how often they repeat and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent using Google's consent message. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account and circle information: while your account or the circle exists.
  • Activity timeline: 90 days. Notification delivery logs: 30 days. Hashed network addresses for code attempts: 7 days.
  • A parent's phone that was never paired to a circle: deleted after 7 days.
  • When you delete your account or a circle, we delete the related personal data from our systems within 30 days. Encrypted backups expire on their normal cycle.
  • Google and Apple keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the app, or without the app on our account deletion page, which explains exactly what is deleted. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent. Most of this can be done in the app. For anything else, email support@o9tech.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal bases (EEA and UK). We use your information to provide the service you asked for (contract); health information with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw by deleting it; security, fraud prevention and crash reporting under our legitimate interests; and ads with your consent where the law requires it.

California. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined by California law, and we request only non-personalised ads.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to each circle is restricted on our servers to its own members. Sign-in sessions are stored in your phone's secure storage. Photos are stored privately and shown through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected users and authorities about a breach as the law requires.

11. Children

Morning Wave is for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
