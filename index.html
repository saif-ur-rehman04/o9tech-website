  • Expert mobile app development for iOS, Android, and cross-platform solutions

  • Blockchain development including smart contracts, DeFi, and NFT solutions
  • Agile development methodology ensuring timely delivery and quality assurance
  • Dedicated support and maintenance services for all deployed solutions

With a team of experienced developers and technology experts, we deliver scalable, secure, and high-performance software solutions tailored to your business needs. We pride ourselves on understanding your vision and translating it into robust digital solutions that not only meet but exceed expectations. From concept to deployment, we ensure seamless integration, optimal performance, and continuous innovation.

Our Services

Why Choose o9Tech for Your Software Development Needs

We combine technical expertise with business acumen to deliver software solutions that drive real results. Our proven track record, cutting-edge technology stack, and client-centric approach make us the ideal partner for your digital transformation journey.

01 What mobile development services do you offer?

We specialize in both native and cross-platform mobile app development. Our native development services include iOS apps using Swift/Objective-C and Android apps using Kotlin/Java. For cross-platform solutions, we use React Native and Flutter to build apps that work seamlessly on both iOS and Android, reducing development time and costs while maintaining native performance.

02 What technologies do you use for web development?

Our web development stack includes modern frameworks and technologies such as React.js, Vue.js, Angular for frontend development, and Node.js, Python (Django/Flask), PHP (Laravel) for backend services. We also work with cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud to ensure scalable, secure, and high-performance web applications.

03 What blockchain development services are available?

We provide comprehensive blockchain development services including smart contract development (Solidity, Rust), DeFi (Decentralized Finance) applications, NFT marketplaces, cryptocurrency wallets, token development, and blockchain consulting. We work with major blockchain platforms like Ethereum, Solana, Polygon, and Hyperledger to build secure and scalable decentralized solutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the topics clients ask us most about timelines, engagement models, and ongoing support.

How long does it take to launch an MVP?

Discovery + design typically takes 2–3 weeks and engineering sprints add 8–12 weeks depending on scope. We present a detailed plan with milestones before writing any code.

What engagement models do you support?

We offer dedicated product squads, staff augmentation, or fixed-scope delivery. Each engagement includes a product lead, tech lead, and QA to ensure accountability.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes. We offer 24/7 monitoring, incident management SLAs, feature squads, and periodic architecture reviews so your product keeps evolving.

How do you keep apps secure and compliant?

Security reviews, automated tests, code scanning, and compliance checklists (SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR) are integrated into every sprint.

Can you collaborate with our in-house team?

Absolutely. We regularly co-build with internal designers, PMs, and engineers. We plug into your tools (Jira, Slack, Linear) to stay fully transparent.
