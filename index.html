How we work

A clear plan, weekly builds, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your goals, users and constraints, then agree on scope, platforms and a realistic timeline. You get a written plan, not a guess.

  2. 02

    Design & architect

    We map the screens and the system behind them: data models, APIs and the stack that fits. Big decisions get made early and on paper.

  3. 03

    Build in sprints

    Working builds every week on TestFlight, Play internal testing or a staging URL. You see real progress and steer as we go.

  4. 04

    Launch & scale

    We handle store submission, monitoring and the first rounds of fixes, then stay on to grow the product with you.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

New apps from scratch, MVPs for startups, and existing apps that need new features or a rescue. About 40% of our recent work is taking over codebases other teams started, then stabilizing and shipping them.

Native or cross-platform: which should we pick?

It depends on your product. Native (Swift, Kotlin) is best when you need deep device features or top performance. Flutter, React Native or Kotlin Multiplatform get you to both stores faster with one codebase. We will recommend one and explain why.

How fast can you ship?

A focused MVP can be live in a few weeks. We took AirLister from zero to the App Store in 14 days. Larger products run in two-week sprints with a working build at the end of each one.

Who will actually work on our project?

Senior engineers who have shipped banking, streaming, messaging and marketplace apps. The architects who scope your project stay involved through launch.

Do you sign NDAs and hand over the code?

Yes. We sign NDAs before detailed talks, and you own the code, designs and accounts. Everything lives in your repositories and store accounts.

Can you support the app after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance, OS updates, monitoring and feature work, either by the month or as needed.

Have a product in mind?

Tell us what you're building. You'll get a reply from a senior engineer within one business day, with honest advice on scope, stack and timeline.
