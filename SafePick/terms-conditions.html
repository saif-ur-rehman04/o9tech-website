01 What SafePick is

SafePick is an informational tool. It reads food product data from the Open Food Facts database and from photos of ingredient labels, compares that data against the allergy and diet profiles you create, and shows you the result with its evidence.

02 What SafePick is not. Please read this section carefully.

SafePick is a helper, not a guarantee.

  • SafePick does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is not a medical device. For questions about allergies, diagnosis, severity, or treatment, talk to a qualified clinician such as your allergist.
  • A green result means the app found no listed allergens in the data available to it. It never means a product is guaranteed safe. Always verify the physical label yourself before buying or eating anything, every time.
  • Product data can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date. Ingredients change without notice. Databases lag behind reformulations. Photographed labels can be misread. Cross-contact ("may contain") information is often incomplete on labels themselves.
  • SafePick is not for emergencies. If you suspect an allergic reaction, follow the emergency plan given by your clinician and contact emergency services. Do not use the app to make decisions during a reaction.
  • If you or a family member are at risk of severe reactions such as anaphylaxis, do not rely on any app, including this one, as your safeguard. Use SafePick as one extra pair of eyes, never the only one.

You remain responsible for what you and your family eat.

03 Your responsibilities

  • Enter profile information accurately and keep it up to date.
  • Verify every product against its physical label regardless of what the app shows.
  • Use reasonably current versions of the app, since allergen dictionaries and fixes ship in updates.
