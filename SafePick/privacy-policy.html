The short version: SafePick has no accounts and no server of its own. Your family's allergy profiles and your scan history live only on your phone. We cannot see them, and we could not hand them over even if asked, because we never have them.

A few narrow things do leave your device, because the app cannot work without them: barcode lookups go to the Open Food Facts database, purchases are processed by Apple or Google together with RevenueCat, and the free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Each is described below, in plain words.

01 Who we are

SafePick is built and operated by Saif Ur Rehman, an independent developer.

Questions about this policy: support@safepick.app.

02 Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app on your phone. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else by SafePick:

  • Family profiles: names or nicknames you enter, chosen colors, allergens, severity settings, and diet preferences. We treat allergy information as sensitive, which is exactly why the app is designed so it never leaves your device.
  • Scan history and saved verdicts.
  • App settings.
  • Label photos: when you photograph an ingredients label, the text is read by on-device software (Google ML Kit running locally). The photo is processed on your phone, is not uploaded, and is not kept by the app after the text has been read.

Note on phone backups: if you use iCloud or Android device backup, your device may include SafePick's local data in your own backup. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Apple's or Google's terms, not ours.

03 Data that leaves your device, and why

Product lookups (Open Food Facts). When you scan a barcode, the app sends that barcode number to the free, non-profit Open Food Facts database to fetch the product's ingredients. The request contains the barcode and ordinary technical request data (such as an IP address, which any internet request includes). It contains nothing about who you are and nothing from your profiles. Open Food Facts privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

Purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat). If you buy SafePick Premium, the payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details. To know that your purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which processes your purchase receipt and an anonymous app-generated identifier. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Ads (free version only). The free version shows a small number of ads through Google AdMob, configured to serve non-personalized ads. Google may process device identifiers and coarse technical data to deliver ads and prevent fraud, under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Ads never appear on scanning or verdict screens, ad providers receive nothing from your profiles or scans, and Premium removes ads entirely.

Support email. If you email us or use the report-a-problem form, we receive what you choose to send and use it only to help you and improve the app's data. Reports are sent anonymously unless you include your contact details.

04 What we never do

  • We never upload, sell, rent, or share your profiles, allergy information, or scan history. We never have them.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and the app does not ask for tracking permission because nothing in it tracks.
  • We do not show ads near safety information, and advertisers get nothing about your allergies.

05 Children

SafePick is a general-audience app. Parents often create profiles for their children; that information stays on the family's own device like everything else. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

06 Your choices and rights

Because your data lives on your device, you are already in control of it:

  • Delete your scan history in the app at any time, or delete everything by uninstalling the app.
  • Export your history (Premium) whenever you want a copy.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights of access, correction, and deletion. In our case there is normally nothing held about you on any server to access or delete, but you can always contact us at support@safepick.app and we will help.

07 Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date, and for meaningful changes we will say so in the app's release notes. We will never quietly change the core promise: profiles stay on your phone.
