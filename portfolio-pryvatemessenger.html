The product

What we built

SwiftObjective-CEnd-to-end encryptionVoIPIn-app purchase

Pryvate is a secure messenger that protects chats, voice and video calls, email and browsing with end-to-end encryption. You can sign up without a phone number.

We engineer the iOS app in Swift and Objective-C: encrypted messaging, VoIP calling, burner numbers, remote data wipe, SSL pinning and in-app purchases.

  • Encrypted chat with self-destruct timers and screenshot alerts
  • Encrypted voice, video and group calls
  • Anonymous in-app phone numbers with no SIM
  • Remote wipe of all app data from another device
  • Built-in secure email and private browsing
