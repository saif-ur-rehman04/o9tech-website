The product

What we built

SwiftUIKitPlivoVoiceKitVoIP

Officetree Phone gives small businesses a local or toll-free number with calling, texting and voicemail, all from the phone in their pocket.

We built the iOS app in Swift and UIKit on PlivoVoiceKit, and added multi-account switching so one person can run calls and texts for more than one business.

  • Business calls from a dedicated number
  • Shared business texting inbox
  • Voicemail sorted by extension
  • Switch between businesses in one app
  • Call routing menus and dark mode
