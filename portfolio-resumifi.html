What we built

Resumifi helps job seekers build professional resumes from templates, with AI that writes job-specific summaries and skill sections.

We led the full-stack build in Next.js, React, Node.js and MongoDB, and integrated OpenAI to draft content. It cut resume creation time by over 60%.

  • Customizable resume templates
  • AI-written summaries and skill sections
  • ATS-friendly output
  • Cover letter and analysis tools
