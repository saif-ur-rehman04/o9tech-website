01 Who we are

Nestora is an AI-powered interior design and home decoration application developed and published by o9Tech, operated by Saif Ur Rehman ("we", "us", "our").

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can reach us directly at support@o9tech.com.

02 Data stored on your device

Nestora is designed so that the vast majority of your data lives exclusively on your local device. The following items are stored locally in the app's sandboxed storage:

  • Room Projects: Photos you take or import of your living room, bedroom, kitchen, office, patio, or other spaces.
  • Generated Designs: AI-rendered interior variations, color palette ideas, furniture recommendations, and decor concepts.
  • Style Preferences & Presets: Your saved interior aesthetics (e.g., Japandi, Scandinavian, Mid-Century Modern, Minimalist, Industrial, Bohemian, Luxury Contemporary).
  • App Settings: Output resolution choices, theme preferences, and subscription status flags.

We do not operate a general user account server or require you to register with an email address or password to use Nestora.

03 Camera & Photo Library permissions

To enable you to redesign your living spaces, Nestora requests explicit iOS permissions:

  • Camera Permission: Used only when you choose to take a live photo of a room or space directly inside the app. Nestora never activates the camera without your direct interaction.
  • Photo Library Access: Used only to let you pick existing room photos from your camera roll or save generated design renderings back to your Photos. Nestora uses Apple's standard system photo picker, ensuring the app only reads the photos you explicitly select.

You can revoke camera or photo library permissions at any time through your device's Settings > Nestora.

04 How AI room generation works

When you select a room photo and choose an interior design theme, the app connects to our secure AI generation infrastructure to produce the requested decor transformation:

  • Transient Transmission: The selected photo and your chosen design parameters (e.g., "Modern Minimalist Living Room with warm lighting") are sent over an encrypted HTTPS/TLS connection.
  • Processing Only: The photo is processed solely to perform the image-to-image decor synthesis, structural edge detection (to preserve your room's walls, windows, and doors), and styling output.
  • No Model Training: Your private home and office photos are never used to train, fine-tune, or improve public AI models without your explicit, separate affirmative consent.
  • Immediate Discard: Once the generated interior images are returned to your device, the uploaded source image is discarded from active processing memory.

05 iCloud backup & synchronization

If you have Apple iCloud enabled on your device, Apple may include Nestora's local database and saved room designs in your personal iCloud Backup or sync them across your Apple devices using Apple CloudKit:

  • Your iCloud storage is encrypted and managed entirely by Apple under your personal Apple ID.
  • We at o9Tech have no access to your personal iCloud credentials, backups, or private files.
  • You can manage or disable iCloud backups for Nestora at any time in your iPhone or iPad Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud.

06 In-app purchases & subscriptions

Nestora offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions (such as Nestora Pro) to unlock unlimited AI generations, high-definition exports, and exclusive design packs:

  • Apple StoreKit: All financial transactions, payments, and credit card processing are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID account. We never receive, process, or store your payment card numbers or billing address.
  • RevenueCat: We utilize RevenueCat to validate purchase receipts and manage subscription entitlements. RevenueCat processes an anonymous, randomized app-user identifier and receipt data to confirm whether your subscription is active. For details, visit RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

07 Third-party services

We believe in minimal third-party integrations. Nestora uses only the essential services required to provide the app:

  • Apple (StoreKit, iCloud, iOS SDKs): Powers in-app payments, device operating system features, and optional iCloud backup. (Apple Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status and receipt validation anonymously. (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)
  • AI Inference Infrastructure: Secure, cloud-hosted GPUs providing ephemeral image synthesis over encrypted endpoints.

Nestora does not include any third-party ad networks, social media tracking pixels, or data brokers.

08 What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal photos, generated interiors, or app usage data.
  • We never track you across other apps or websites. Nestora does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission because we do not engage in cross-app tracking.
  • We never claim ownership of the photos you take of your home or workplace.

09 Children's privacy

Nestora is intended for general audiences and home decorators, homeowners, renters, and design enthusiasts aged 13 and above (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@o9tech.com and we will immediately take steps to remove it.

10 Your choices & data rights

Because your projects and photos are stored on your device, you possess total control over your information:

  • Deleting Content: You can delete any saved room project, photo, or generated design directly within the app at any time.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Deleting the Nestora app from your device immediately permanently deletes all local application data and cached renderings.
  • Statutory Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA): Depending on your location, you may have statutory rights to request information regarding data processing, access, or erasure. Since we do not maintain accounts or store identifiable user profiles on central servers, there is typically no personal record linked to your identity. However, for any privacy inquiries, reach out to us at support@o9tech.com.
