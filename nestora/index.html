Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. Nestora works straight out of the box — every device gets 3 free designs, and Pro or credit packs are bought with the Apple ID already on your device. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional and only carries purchased credits to your other devices. If you do sign in, you can delete the account and everything stored with it from Profile → Delete Account.

Will it keep my room's real layout?

Yes — that is the point. Nestora reads the structure of the photo, so walls, windows, doors and proportions stay where they are. What changes is furniture, finishes, colour and light.

What happens to my photos?

They are sent over an encrypted connection purely to generate the design you asked for, and they are never used to train public AI models. No ad networks, no tracking pixels, no data brokers. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.

What counts as one design?

One generated image. Trying the same room in a second style uses a second design, which is why most people move to Pro once they start comparing options seriously.

How do I cancel?

In Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the next renewal. Cancelling keeps Pro active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
