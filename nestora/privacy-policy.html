Privacy Policy

Effective: [date you publish this]Last updated: [same date]Applies to the Nestora apps for iPhone and Android

Nestora turns photos of your spaces into AI-generated redesigns. That means we handle something personal — pictures of your home — so this policy is written to be read, not skimmed. It explains what we collect, what we do with it, what we will never do with it, and how you stay in control. It covers the Nestora app on both the App Store and Google Play.

At a glance

What we collect

The photos you choose to redesign, your purchase status, usage analytics, and an advertising identifier used for the optional rewarded ads. If you choose to sign in, we also receive your Apple or Google sign-in identity.

Where your designs live

On your device. Your photos and the designs we generate are stored locally in the app and are not uploaded to a Nestora account or synced between devices.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, use your photos to train AI models, or read anything from your photo library beyond the images you pick.

Your control

Delete any design in the app, delete your whole account from Profile → Delete Account, and email support@o9tech.com to exercise any privacy right.

Your photos and AI processing

Redesigns are generated by a third-party AI service, not on your phone. Here is exactly what happens when you tap Generate:

  1. The App resizes and re-encodes your photo. This step removes EXIF metadata, including any GPS coordinates the original file contained.
  2. The photo is first checked automatically to confirm it shows a physical space and matches the category you picked. Photos that are too dark, blurry, or not of a space are rejected before any redesign runs.
  3. The photo and your chosen settings are sent over an encrypted connection to Google’s Gemini API, which generates the redesign. Depending on your app version, this request is sent either directly from your device or routed through our own backend at Supabase so that no API credentials are stored inside the app.
  4. The generated design is returned to the App and saved on your device alongside the original, so you can compare them.
Your photos are not used to train AI models. We do not use your images or generated designs to train or improve machine-learning models, and we do not publish them. Google processes images submitted through the paid Gemini API only to fulfil the request and does not use them to train its models; see Google’s Gemini API terms for its retention and abuse-monitoring practices.

We do not keep a copy of your photos or designs on our servers. They remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.

You own your photos and the designs we generate for you — see the Terms of Service. Please do not upload photos that show identifiable people, documents, screens containing personal information, or property you do not have permission to photograph. Nestora is designed for spaces, not people, and our checks may reject such images.

Advertising and the advertising ID

Nestora shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You are never shown an ad unless you choose to watch one to earn a free generation credit. We show no banner ads, no interstitials and no ads anywhere else in the App.

To serve, cap and measure those ads, and to detect invalid traffic, AdMob uses your device’s advertising identifier and related device and ad-interaction data. This identifier is a resettable ID that your operating system provides, and it is shared with Google as an independent controller for these purposes.

Analytics and session replay

We use PostHog, hosted in the European Union, to understand how the App is used so we can improve it. PostHog records:

  • Product events — for example starting onboarding, choosing a space and style, starting and finishing a redesign, generation failures, viewing the paywall, and watching a rewarded ad.
  • Screen views and app lifecycle events — which screens you open and when the App starts and stops.
  • Session replay — a reconstruction of your interactions with the App’s interface, so we can see where people get stuck. [Confirm your PostHog project settings before publishing: text inputs are masked, and IP addresses are discarded.]

If you are signed in, these events are linked to your account identifier. If you are not signed in, they are linked to a pseudonymous identifier generated for your installation. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not combine it with your photos.

How we use information

  • Provide the service — generate designs, check that a photo shows the kind of space you selected, and keep your designs available in the App.
  • Manage your plan — count free generations, verify subscriptions and credit packs with Apple and Google, apply your monthly allowance, and prevent quota abuse.
  • Show optional rewarded ads — so you can earn free credits without paying, and measure whether those ads worked.
  • Keep Nestora working and safe — diagnose failures, monitor performance, detect fraud and misuse, and enforce our Terms and content rules.
  • Improve the product — understand which spaces, styles and features are used most, and where people get stuck, so we can prioritise improvements.
  • Communicate with you — respond to support requests and send essential service messages. We only send marketing email if you opt in, and every one includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Comply with the law — keep the transaction records tax and accounting rules require, and respond to lawful requests.

We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects using automated processing.

Device permissions

The App asks for these permissions only when you use the related feature, and each can be changed at any time in your device settings:

  • Camera — to photograph a space. Frames are used only for the photo you take; nothing is recorded in the background.
  • Photos — we use the system photo picker, which lets you choose specific images without giving the App access to your whole library. Saving a design needs add-only permission.
  • Notifications — optional. Nestora sends only local notifications, scheduled on your device to remind you about a design in progress or an unused credit. No push server is involved and no device token leaves your phone.

Security

All data travels over TLS-encrypted connections. Your photos and designs stay in the App’s private storage on your device. Sign-in tokens are stored in your device’s secure credential storage. Access to production systems is limited to staff who need it and protected by multi-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you and any relevant authority as the law requires.

International transfers

Our providers are located in the European Union and the United States. If you use Nestora from the EEA, UK or Switzerland, your data is transferred outside those areas. We rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and the EU–US, UK and Swiss–US Data Privacy Frameworks where a provider is certified. You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards by emailing us.

Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and delete your designs — every photo and design is on your device. Delete them individually in the Archive, use Reset All Saved Data in Profile, or uninstall the App.
  • Delete your account — Profile → Delete Account removes your account and the credit record attached to it. This is immediate and cannot be undone. Unused credits are forfeited.

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you additionally have the rights to restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. We will respond to requests within one month.

To exercise any right, email support@o9tech.com from the address on your account, or include enough information for us to verify that the request is genuinely yours. We will never discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

California and other US states

This section supplements the rest of the policy for residents of California (CCPA/CPRA) and of other US states with comprehensive privacy laws, including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon.

Categories of personal information collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (email address, account and device identifiers, advertising identifier, IP address); commercial information (purchase and subscription history); internet or other electronic network activity (usage, session replay and diagnostic data); and visual information (the photos you submit for a redesign, which are processed and not retained by us). Sources: you, your device, and Apple or Google. Purposes: as described in How we use information. Disclosed to: the providers listed in Who we share it with.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, under California and several other state laws, using an advertising identifier to show personalised advertising can count as “sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising”. To the extent that applies, you can opt out at any time by resetting or opting out of your device advertising ID, as described in Advertising, and we treat Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out request. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

You have the rights to know, access, correct, delete and port your personal information, to opt out of sale and sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them, all as described in Your rights and choices.

Children

Nestora is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old, or 16 where local law sets a higher age of digital consent, to use Nestora, and old enough to enter a binding contract to make purchases — otherwise a parent or guardian must do so on your behalf. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below these ages; if you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
