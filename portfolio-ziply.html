What we built

SwiftKotlinJavaNode.js

Ziply is an on-demand courier for local businesses. A driver picks up a package within the hour and takes it straight to the drop-off.

We built the native iOS and Android apps on a Node.js backend, from ordering and pickup to live delivery status.

  • Place a delivery in a few taps
  • Pickup within an hour
  • Live delivery status
  • Small and large packages
