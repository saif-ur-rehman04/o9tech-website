Your privacy matters to us. AppBlocker is designed to help you manage your screen time — not to monetize your personal data. We do not sell your data to third parties, ever.

Category Examples Required?

Usage Data App open counts, feature interactions, onboarding step progress Yes

03 Screen Time & Device Data

AppBlocker uses Apple's Screen Time / Family Controls framework to help you block apps and set usage limits. This requires the Screen Time permission on your device.

Screen Time data (which apps you block, time ranges, schedules) is processed locally on your device using Apple's ScreenTime API. This data is never transmitted to our servers. We do not have access to which specific apps you block.

Your estimated screen time guess entered during onboarding is stored locally and optionally synced to your account solely to personalise your experience.

05 Data Storage & Security

Your account data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure, which uses industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Blocking schedules and screen time data are stored locally on your device and protected by iOS's built-in sandboxing.

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If you use Sign in with Apple with the Hide My Email option, Apple provides a private relay address. We store and use that relay address as your email — we never receive or store your real Apple ID email in that case.

06 Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Anonymised, aggregated usage analytics (which cannot identify you) may be retained indefinitely to help us improve the app.

Local data (blocking schedules, screen time) is deleted when you uninstall the app or reset the app's data through iOS Settings.

08 Children's Privacy

AppBlocker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete that information.

If you are between 13 and 18, please review this policy with a parent or guardian before using the app.
