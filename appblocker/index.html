Features

Everything you need to stay focused

AppBlock uses Apple's Screen Time API to block apps at the system level — no VPN, no tricks, no workarounds.

Quick Block

Start a blocking session instantly. Pick your apps, set a timer, and focus starts in seconds. No setup needed.

Time Schedules

Create recurring rules for specific days and hours. Block social media during work hours, automatically, every day.

Location Blocking

Automatically block apps when you arrive at a place — your office, gym, or library. Leave and they unlock.

Open Limit

Allow yourself to open Instagram only 3 times today. Once you hit the limit, it's blocked until tomorrow.

Usage Limit

Set a daily time budget per app. Spend 30 minutes on YouTube, and it's blocked for the rest of the day.

Widgets

Add AppBlock widgets to your Home Screen. See active blocks and remaining time at a glance.

How It Works

Up and blocking in 3 steps

No technical setup. No config files. Just open the app and start blocking.

1

Download & Sign In

Install AppBlock for free. Sign in with Apple or Google — or skip and browse as a guest.

2

Grant Screen Time Access

Allow AppBlock to use Apple's Screen Time API. This is what lets it block apps at the system level.

3

Set Your Rules & Go

Pick your apps, choose a blocking mode — Quick Block, Schedule, Location, or Limits — and you're done.
