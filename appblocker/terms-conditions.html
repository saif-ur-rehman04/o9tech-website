Important: All AppBlocker purchases are processed exclusively through the Apple App Store. o9tech does not directly process payments or issue refunds — all refund requests must go through Apple.

01 How Subscriptions Work

AppBlocker offers auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly and annual) managed entirely through the Apple App Store and RevenueCat. By subscribing, you agree to the following:

Plan Billing Cycle Auto-Renews
Monthly Premium Every 30 days Yes, unless cancelled
Annual Premium Every 365 days Yes, unless cancelled

Your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each billing period and your Apple ID will be charged unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You can manage and cancel your subscription at any time through your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

02 Free Trials

AppBlocker may offer free trial periods for new subscribers. During a free trial:

You will not be charged until the trial period ends. If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged at all. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your subscription will automatically begin and your Apple ID will be charged the applicable subscription price.

Free trials are available to new subscribers only. If you have previously subscribed to AppBlocker Premium, you may not be eligible for a free trial.

03 Refund Eligibility

Because all purchases are processed by Apple, refund eligibility is governed by Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions. As a general guide:

Situation Likely Eligible?
Accidental purchase or duplicate charge Yes
Charged after forgetting to cancel free trial Possibly
App did not work as described / technical issue we cannot resolve Possibly
Changed your mind after using the subscription Generally No
Partial period refund (cancelled mid-cycle) Generally No
Annual plan after significant time has passed Generally No

Final refund decisions rest with Apple. We are unable to override Apple's decisions regarding App Store purchases.

04 How to Request a Refund

Follow these steps to request a refund directly through Apple:

1
Go to reportaproblem.apple.com

Sign in with the Apple ID used to purchase AppBlocker.

2
Find your AppBlocker purchase

Locate the subscription or in-app purchase you want refunded.

3
Select "Request a Refund"

Choose the reason that best describes your situation and submit.

4
Wait for Apple's decision

Apple typically responds within a few days. You'll receive an email confirmation.

Alternatively, on your iPhone go to Settings → Your Name → Purchase History, tap the purchase, and select "Report a Problem."

05 Apple's Refund Process

Apple handles all payment processing and refund decisions independently. o9tech has no ability to access your payment details, initiate refunds on Apple's behalf, or guarantee refund approval.

For more information on Apple's refund policies visit: support.apple.com/en-us/118223

If Apple approves a refund, the amount will be returned to your original payment method. Timing depends on your bank or card issuer and typically takes 5–10 business days.

06 What Happens After a Refund

If a refund is granted by Apple:

Your AppBlocker Premium access will be revoked and your account will revert to the free/guest tier. Any Premium features — including advanced blocking schedules and sync — will no longer be accessible. Your blocking data and settings remain on your device but will not be synced across devices.

You are welcome to re-subscribe at any time after a refund.

07 Exceptional Circumstances

If you experienced a significant technical issue that prevented you from using AppBlocker Premium and Apple did not resolve your refund request, please contact us directly. While we cannot process refunds ourselves, we may be able to:

Provide complimentary Premium access for a period to compensate for downtime, escalate documented technical issues with Apple on your behalf, or offer account credits toward future purchases where applicable.

These remedies are offered at o9tech's sole discretion and are not guaranteed.
