Hookah Butler let guests at partner lounges, bars and restaurants order from their phone and have it served at their table.

We built the native Android app with Google Maps: nearby venue search, menu browsing and custom orders.

  • Find partner lounges nearby
  • Map and list views
  • Filter by rating or distance
  • Customize and order to your table
