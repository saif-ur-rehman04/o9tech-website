The product

What we built

SwiftUIKitREST APIsPayments

The catalogue app of Pakistani textile maker Kohinoor Mills lets trade buyers browse close to a thousand fabrics and order samples from anywhere.

We built the iOS app in Swift and UIKit: the product catalogue, advanced search and filters, cart, payment integration and order tracking.

  • Browse by season, category and lookbook
  • Filter by weave, weight, blend and finish
  • Order samples as fabric or stitched pieces
  • Scan sample barcodes at trade shows
  • Order history with push updates
