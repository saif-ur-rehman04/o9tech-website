The product

What we built

SwiftUIKitOnboarding SDKBanking security

The Boubyan App is the main banking app of Boubyan Bank in Kuwait, covering accounts, cards, deposits, financing and transfers. It holds a 4.4 rating from 40,000+ App Store reviews.

We worked on the iOS app in Swift and UIKit: a secure onboarding SDK plus account, transfer and payment features built to strict banking rules.

  • Open an account or deposit in the app
  • Card PIN changes and finance installments
  • Worldwide transfers and payment links
  • Cardless cash at Boubyan ATMs
  • Chat with the bank's digital assistant
