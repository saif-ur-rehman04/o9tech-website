What we do

How we help

For most startups, one shared codebase gets you to both stores faster and keeps costs down. We pick the right tool for your product: Flutter for rich custom UI, React Native and Expo when you have a web team, and Kotlin Multiplatform when you want native UI with shared logic.

  • Flutter apps with custom design systems and smooth animation
  • React Native and Expo apps, including EAS builds and OTA updates
  • Kotlin Multiplatform with Compose Multiplatform UI
  • Real-time features: live video, chat, auctions and sync
  • Taking over and stabilizing apps other teams started
