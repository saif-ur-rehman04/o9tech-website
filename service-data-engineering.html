FAQ

Data engineering questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What is the difference between data engineering and data science?

Data engineering builds the pipes and the storage: it collects, cleans and organizes data so it is reliable. Data science and AI then use that data to find patterns, make predictions or power features. We do both, and data engineering usually comes first.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Yes. We connect to your existing databases, apps and SaaS tools, and pick a warehouse that fits your size and budget, from PostgreSQL to BigQuery or Snowflake.

How do you keep our data safe?

Data stays in your own cloud accounts, access is limited to the people who need it, and we sign NDAs before detailed talks. You own the code, the pipelines and the accounts.
